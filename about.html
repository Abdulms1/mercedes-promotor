About Us

As an Authorized Dealership of Mercedes-Benz, PRO Motor is committed to delivering a premium ownership experience that goes beyond the vehicle itself. For us, it is about quality, trust, and building long-term relationships with every customer.

PT. Panji Rama Otomotif (PRO Motor) was established in 1997 in Simprug, South Jakarta, and has grown into a trusted Mercedes-Benz dealer in Indonesia[cite: 6]. Appointed as an Authorized Mercedes-Benz UNIMOG dealer in 2010 and granted Dealer Exclusivity in 2014 now known as Mercedes-Benz Certified Pre-Owned, PRO Motor currently operates two dealerships across Jakarta and Tangerang, covering passenger cars, and certified pre-owned units[cite: 6].
